# ScholarSathi — AI-Powered Scholarship Discovery & Eligibility Portal

ScholarSathi is an AI-powered scholarship discovery and eligibility portal designed specifically for students across India. It matches students with central, state, corporate (CSR), and philanthropic scholarships based on their personal, socio-economic, and academic profiles.
